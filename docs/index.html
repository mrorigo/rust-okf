01 / The premise

Search should feel like a superpower.

Most knowledge systems make you choose between keyword recall and semantic vibes. rust-okf gives you both: BM25 for the exact stuff, embeddings for the adjacent stuff, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion to bring the useful stuff to the top.

It is deliberately small, deliberately local, and built in Rust for the moments when your index needs to be fast, safe, and still yours.

02 / Under the hood

A small engine.
Big signal.

A manifest-driven store keeps ingestion, ranking, persistence, and recovery cleanly separated. Less magic. More control.

03 / The storage engine

Writes land.
Nothing gets lost.

rust-okf treats knowledge like a system that deserves a paper trail. Every update becomes an immutable segment. The manifest points at what is live. The journal remembers what happened when the lights go out.
